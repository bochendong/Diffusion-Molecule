\section{External Benchmark Snapshot}
\label{sec:external-results-snapshot}

Table~\ref{tab:sketchmol-fair40} is the currently completed external
comparison.  Both methods receive forty generation attempts per condition;
the score is the fraction of conditions for which at least one generated
molecule satisfies all active properties.  MolProgram is competitive for
low-order programs, but its average is lower because the gap grows for five-
to seven-property programs.  We therefore do not claim state of the art from
this table.

Table~\ref{tab:moledit-table1} gives the source-conditioned editing result
under the MolEdit-Instruct Table~1 metric.  The direct row evaluates the first
generated candidate.  The 20-candidate rows use a property-aware selector and
are explicitly marked assisted; they must not be presented as one-shot
generation.  Group-RL yields a small matched-seed improvement over SFT at the
relaxed similarity threshold, but neither checkpoint succeeds at the strict
0.65 threshold.  Editing presently supports a shared interface, not a
competitive external-result claim.

The remaining main external comparison is MuMOInstruct, evaluated with the
official 20-candidate success rate (SR), similarity of successful edits, and
relative improvement (RI).  The reported GeLLM3O targets are 76.8\% SR for
in-distribution tasks and 90.8\% for out-of-distribution tasks.  We leave the
MolProgram cells out of the manuscript until the same-oracle evaluation is
complete rather than mixing these metrics with an internal similarity proxy.
