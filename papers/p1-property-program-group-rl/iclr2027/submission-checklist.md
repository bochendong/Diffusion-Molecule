# ICLR 2027 submission checklist

Official sources:

- author guide: https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2027/AuthorGuidelines
- call for papers: https://www.iclr.cc/Conferences/2027/CallForPapers
- AI policy: https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2027/AIPolicyForAuthors
- OpenReview venue: https://openreview.net/group?id=ICLR.cc/2027/Conference

## Deadlines

- abstract: September 18, 2026, 11:59 PM AoE;
- full paper: September 25, 2026, AoE;
- author list is frozen after the abstract deadline;
- reviews: November 5, 2026;
- author-reviewer discussion: November 5--18, 2026.

## Format and policy

- anonymous double-blind submission;
- ICLR 2027 official style files, vendored unchanged in this directory;
- at most 9 pages of main text at initial submission;
- references do not count toward the limit;
- appendices after references are unlimited, but reviewers need not read them;
- required AI use statement, excluded from page limit;
- recommended ethics and reproducibility statements, excluded from page limit;
- every author needs an up-to-date OpenReview profile;
- no authors may be added after the abstract deadline;
- verify reciprocal-reviewer and paper-quota requirements before abstract submission.

## P1 scientific gates

- [ ] full paired 6,000-condition 2p-to-7p result synchronized from Nibi;
- [ ] machine-generated 2p-to-7p complexity figure;
- [ ] machine-generated budget-scaling figure;
- [ ] raw versus selected validity audit included;
- [ ] property-program-only ablation;
- [ ] curriculum ablation available or limitation strengthened;
- [ ] exact train/evaluation overlap audit;
- [ ] parameter count and compute accounting;
- [ ] all preliminary labels removed or deliberately retained with justification;
- [ ] every numerical statement traced to `p1_paper_main_table.csv`;
- [ ] anonymized supplementary code and paths inspected for identity leaks.
